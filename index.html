FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Does NeoReception.AI sound like a robot?
Absolutely not. NeoReception.AI uses the latest neural TTS (text-to-speech) voices that are virtually indistinguishable from a real person. You can also customize the voice, tone, name, and personality to perfectly match your brand.
What happens when a caller needs a human?
Neo detects when a caller needs a live agent and seamlessly transfers them — optionally with a spoken summary of the conversation so your team never has to ask the caller to repeat themselves.
Can I keep my existing phone number?
Yes. Simply set up a call forward from your existing number to your NeoReception number. Your callers will never know a thing changed — except that someone always answers.
How does setup work?
Setup is simple and handled by our team. After a quick discovery call, we configure your AI persona, connect your phone number, and integrate your calendar — most businesses are live within 24 hours with zero technical work on your end.
How much does NeoReception.AI cost?
Pricing is customized based on your call volume and business needs. Most clients find it costs a fraction of a part-time receptionist — with better coverage. Book a call and we'll put together a plan that fits your budget.
