Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2025

NeoReception.AI ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients and their callers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our AI receptionist services.

By using NeoReception.AI, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

1. Information We Collect

From Business Clients

  • Name, email address, and phone number during account setup
  • Business name, address, and industry type
  • Calendar and scheduling preferences
  • Payment and billing information (processed securely via third-party providers)
  • Custom AI configuration settings (voice, tone, scripts, FAQs)

From Callers

  • Phone numbers of inbound and outbound calls
  • Call recordings and transcriptions
  • Information voluntarily shared during calls (name, appointment details, inquiries)
  • Call metadata (date, time, duration)

Automatically Collected

  • Website usage data, browser type, and IP address
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Device and operating system information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve our AI receptionist services
  • Train and customize your AI persona based on your business configuration
  • Send call summaries, transcripts, and lead notifications
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service updates
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized use

We do not sell your data or your callers' data to third parties.

3. Call Recording & Transcription

NeoReception.AI records and transcribes calls as part of its core service. By using our service, you represent that you will comply with all applicable laws regarding call recording disclosure in your jurisdiction. In many states and countries, you are required to notify callers that calls may be recorded.

We recommend including a brief disclosure in your AI receptionist's greeting (e.g., "This call may be recorded for quality purposes."). We can configure this for you during onboarding.

Call recordings and transcripts are retained for 90 days by default. Extended retention may be available on certain plans.

All third-party service providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only to provide services on our behalf.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit

  • Access controls and role-based permissions

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your subscription is active and for up to 30 days after termination. Call recordings are kept for 90 days unless you request earlier deletion or have a plan with extended retention. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
